This tutorial walks you from a blank signup form to a working space where you land as an admin, then gives you a quick tour of the dashboard and left navigation so you know where everything lives. It takes about five minutes.

By the end you will have a space of your own, an admin membership inside it, and a mental map of the sidebar.

## Before you start

You need an email address and a password of at least 8 characters. If someone has already set up a space for your group and given you an invite code, skip ahead to [Joining an existing space](#joining-an-existing-space) instead.

## Step 1: Create your account

1. Go to `/signup`.
2. Under **Create your account**, fill in **Full Name**, **Email**, and **Password** (minimum 8 characters).
3. Under **What are you doing?**, click the **Create a Space** card.
4. Click **Continue →**.

## Step 2: Name your space

Now you are on the **Set up your Space** step.

1. Enter a **Space Name**, such as `HeatSync Labs`. As you type, a **Slug** is filled in for you automatically — it is the lowercase, letters-and-numbers-only handle for your space (for example `heatsynclabs`). You can edit it.
2. Optionally enter a **City / Location**.
3. Read the note: you will be the admin of this space, and default channels (general, announcements, ops) are created for you.
4. Click **Create Space →**.

Depending on how email confirmation is configured, one of two things happens:

- You go straight to `/dashboard`, already signed in.
- You are sent to `/signup/confirm` to verify your email first. Open the link in that email, and you will land on the dashboard afterward.

That is it — your space exists. As the founder you are set up as an **admin** on the **plus** tier with a **current** status, and you skip the member onboarding flow entirely.

## Step 3: Get your bearings on the dashboard

The first time you open `/dashboard` in an empty space, you see a **"Let's set up your space"** panel instead of a grid of zeros. It offers four first steps:

| Step | Where it goes |
| --- | --- |
| Invite or add members | `/members` |
| Set up onboarding | `/customize` |
| Create your first task or chore | `/tasks` |
| Connect payments | `/settings` |

That panel disappears on its own once there is real activity. After that, the dashboard shows a stat row — **Active Members**, **Dues Current**, **Open Tasks**, **Unlinked Payments** — plus quick chores, active projects, who is present now, and recent activity. The **Quick Task** button in the header jumps to `/tasks`.

## Step 4: Explore the left navigation

The sidebar groups every screen into sections. As an admin you see all of them:

- **Workspace** — Dashboard, Tasks & Chores, Projects, Ops & Facilities, Comms, Forum
- **Governance** — Proposals, Incidents, Policies
- **Access & Resources** — Classes, Equipment, Doors, Forms
- **People** — Members, Attendance, Contacts, Recruitment
- **Finance** — Payments, Financials
- **Account** — My membership
- **Admin** — Customize, Settings, Import / Sync, and the management screens for forms, certifications, classes, equipment, and doors

The **Admin** section only appears for admins, board members, or members you have granted a specific manage permission. There is also a **Jump to…** button at the top of the sidebar (or press ⌘K) that opens a command palette for fast navigation.

A good next stop is [inviting your first members](/members), or `/customize` to shape what new members see when they join.

## Joining an existing space

If you were given an invite code instead of starting a new space:

1. Go to `/signup` (or open the `/signup?invite=CODE` link your admin shared, which prefills the code for you).
2. Fill in your account details and click the **Join a Space** card, then **Continue →**.
3. Enter the **Invite Code** — it looks like `HSL-2025-XXXX`. Ask your space admin for it if you do not have one; they can find the space's default code under Settings.
4. Click **Join Space →**.

You join as a **member**. If the space requires approval (the default), your status starts as **unverified** and an admin approves you before you gain full access — until then the dashboard is still yours to look around. If the space does not require approval, you are **current** right away.

## What you did

You created (or joined) a space, landed with the right role, and learned where the dashboard and sidebar put things. From here, follow the dashboard's setup steps to add members, build your onboarding flow, and start capturing tasks.
